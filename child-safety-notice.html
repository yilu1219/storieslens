Founding Beta Child Safety Notice

StoriesLens begins with an adult-owned, invitation-only family account. A child does not open an independent account in the founding beta.

What a parent controls

The adult account owner chooses the storage region, decides what a young creator may submit, reviews generated material, records permission for private media and sharing, and can revoke invitations or delete the account.

What the beta does not allow

No public story discovery, stranger co-creation, direct messaging, targeted advertising, real-person photo transformation, precise location collection, identity documents, school records, or unrestricted public profiles.

Private collaboration

Sharing uses unlisted invitations that expire. A child’s project remains private until the adult account owner records approval. Approval can be revoked, which also revokes active project invitations.

Report a concern

Do not use the service for an emergency. Stop sharing the link and contact safety@storieslens.com. A confirmed production response process and responsible adult must be assigned before launch.

Draft date: September 14, 2026.

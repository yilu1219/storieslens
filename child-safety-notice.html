Founding Beta Child Safety Notice

StoriesLens begins with an adult-owned, invitation-only family account. A child does not open an independent account in the founding beta.

What a parent controls

The adult account owner chooses the storage region, decides what a young creator may submit, reviews generated material, records permission for private media and sharing, and can revoke photo permission, permanently delete a project or delete the account.

Personal-photo protection

Safe personal or family photos are allowed only after the adult account owner confirms permission and acknowledges regional processing. The browser removes location and camera metadata before a working copy is sent. The photo remains private, is not placed in public discovery and is not used to train StoriesLens models. Revoking photo permission deletes media saved under that permission.

What the beta does not allow

No public story discovery, stranger co-creation, direct messaging, targeted advertising, precise location collection, identity documents, school records, visible contact information, non-consensual photo use, sexual content, graphic violence, or unrestricted public profiles.

Private collaboration

Sharing uses unlisted invitations that expire. A child’s project remains private until the adult account owner records approval. Approval can be revoked, which also revokes active project invitations.

Report a concern

Do not use the service for an emergency. Stop sharing the link and contact safety@storieslens.com. A confirmed production response process and responsible adult must be assigned before launch.

## Adopted teaching abstractions

StoriesLens does not store the guide's examples, passages, prompts, or sentence starters. Yu uses only these independently written teaching decisions:

1. Separate the creator's language-learning target from the story or craft target so that one does not erase the other.
2. Let a learner rehearse an idea orally or organize it visually before drafting when that lowers the barrier to expression.
3. Teach only a small number of words that are necessary to communicate the creator's intended meaning in the current task.
4. Offer an empty organizer, choice of relationship, or fill-in-the-blank frame; never produce finished prose for the creator.
5. Fade support after use by asking the creator to restate, expand, or revise independently.

## Evidence caution

The guide rates its recommendation for integrated oral and written language instruction as strong evidence, while its recommendation for regular structured written-language opportunities is rated minimal evidence and partly reflects panel judgment. Yu must not flatten these into one certainty claim.

# IES / WWC writing practice guides — source record

Retrieved: 2026-09-13

## Elementary (grades 1–6)

- Institution: What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education.
- Original PDF: https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/WWC_Elem_Writing_PG_Dec182018.pdf
- Release: June 2012; revised October 2018.
- Rights evidence: PDF page 4 states that the report is in the public domain and requests citation.
- Credibility: federal practice guide; recommendations carry explicit evidence ratings and were prepared with a named expert panel.
- Stable factual extract (paraphrased): provide regular writing time; explicitly teach a flexible writing process for multiple purposes; build transcription and sentence fluency; create an engaged community in which writers choose, collaborate, receive feedback, and publish.

## Secondary (grades 6–12)

- Institution: What Works Clearinghouse, Institute of Education Sciences, U.S. Department of Education.
- Original PDF: https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/Docs/PracticeGuide/wwc_secondary_writing_110116.pdf
- Release: November 2016; revised December 2019.
- Rights evidence: PDF page 4 states that the report is in the public domain and requests citation.
- Credibility: federal practice guide combining research review, expert panel judgment, evidence ratings, and external review.
- Stable factual extract (paraphrased): teach strategies through a Model–Practice–Reflect cycle; connect reading and writing around visible text features; use formative assessment to select the next instructional move and feedback level.

No endorsement is claimed. The local training module is an original instructional abstraction, not a reproduction of examples, tables, or prose from either guide.

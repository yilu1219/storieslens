## Adopted teaching abstraction

Yu may scan a draft for recurring grammar patterns, choose the pattern that most affects meaning, name it in child-friendly language, and let the creator repair their own sentence. If a creator asks for a full check, Yu may list the detected items, group repeated instances under one pattern, and teach only a small number at a time.

## Adopted teaching abstractions

StoriesLens stores no source passages or textbook examples. Yu may use an independently written feedback sequence: the creator states the intended reader effect; a listener reports what they understood and where they became uncertain; comments are separated into observation, question, and suggestion; conflicting suggestions are compared against the creator’s purpose; and the creator chooses one high-leverage revision before reading the passage again.

## Boundaries

- Feedback is evidence for a decision, not a vote that overrules the creator.
- Do not replace the creator’s wording with a finished passage.
- Do not criticize identity, dialect, accent, or multilingual expression.
- Do not present an unranked list of every possible edit.
- Do not copy textbook examples or claim University of Hawai‘i or CCSS endorsement.

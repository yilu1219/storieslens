Creator trust · Service principles

Every creator stays in control.

StoriesLens welcomes every age. AI can unlock imagination without becoming the author, while younger creators receive additional privacy and collaboration safeguards.

Clear product boundaries: these safeguards describe how the service is designed; they are not a claim of legal certification. A parent, guardian, or educator should supervise young creators’ accounts, invitations, sharing, and purchases.

01 · AUTHORSHIP

AI asks; the human writes

Story Coach gives one question or a short direction at a time. It does not insert finished story sentences into a creator’s draft. Every human contribution keeps its creator credit.

02 · COLLABORATION

Invite-only, not stranger matching

Solo projects begin private. Story Squads use codes shared by a family, friend, or teacher. There are no open direct messages and no public child-discovery directory.

03 · ORIGINALITY

Transform inspiration; do not copy it

Story DNA asks what feeling or idea stayed with the creator, then requires a meaningful change and originality shift. Children should not paste copyrighted books, scripts, or long passages.

04 · DATA MINIMIZATION

Collect less by design

Projects and media are private by default and use authenticated service endpoints. Anonymous funnel events do not include story text or names. When AI generation is requested, only the relevant draft may be sent to the configured AI provider for processing.

05 · SHARING

Private before public

  • Use a first name or nickname, not contact information.
  • A grown-up reviews any public share or production use.
  • Creators can save locally before publishing.
06 · PURCHASES

Writing first; production second

Creators can explore writing before deciding to pay. Adults purchase directly; a guardian completes purchases for minors. Visual and film production costs are shown separately.

07 · SAFE CONTENT

Strict for every age

Sexual, graphic violent, self-harm, hateful, and dangerous instructional content is blocked for adults and minors. Media is not released when safety review is unavailable.

08 · REAL-PERSON PHOTOS

Accepted for private creation and video

Creators may use a personal or family photo when they have permission. The device removes location and camera metadata first. Safe photos may be saved inside the creator’s private project and used to make a book or video; they are never made public automatically. Sexual content, graphic violence, identity documents, contact details and other protected information remain blocked.

给家长与老师

StoriesLens 的原则是：创作者始终是作者，AI 只负责提问和引导；独立作品默认私密，共创小队仅限邀请，不提供陌生人匹配或公开私信；不要上传完整书籍、剧本、真实住址、联系方式或其他敏感信息。内测版允许使用已获本人或监护人同意的真人照片：设备会先删除定位和拍摄信息；通过内容与隐私检查后，照片可保存到创作者的私密项目并用于制作书或视频，但不会自动公开。色情内容、血腥暴力、身份证件、联系方式及其他敏感资料仍会被拦截。未成年人账号的邀请、分享、AI 生成和购买应由家长、监护人或教师监督。
